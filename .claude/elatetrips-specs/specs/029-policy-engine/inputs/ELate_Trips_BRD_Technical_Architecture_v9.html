1 Document purpose and control

This document defines the consolidated development scope for ELate Trips. The platform will help travelers plan around the purpose of a trip and compare suitable transport, accommodation, packages and local services in one workspace. It also defines dedicated organization journeys and the shared partner, sales and administration workflows required to fulfill them.

2 Customer problem and business outcomes

Customers currently piece together tickets, stays, local travel and experiences across multiple places. A cheap ticket may produce an inconvenient whole trip; a hotel may not suit a family, a restful break or a milestone. ELate Trips will use the customer’s purpose and a few essential inputs to propose practical complete trip options while allowing the customer to buy only the components they need.

  • Reduce repeated searching and re-entry of route, dates and traveler details.
  • Show why an option suits leisure, relaxation, family, celebrations, pilgrimage or a shared group.
  • Compare separate flight/bus/travel and hotel choices alongside bus packages and private complete packages.
  • Connect a customer-approved plan with supplier evidence and a clear operational next step.
Outcome measureDefinition and baseline approach
Planning effortMedian customer turns and time from accepted essentials to first eligible option; measure after instrumented pilot, no invented improvement claim.
Commercial clarityShare of proposed trips with reconciled component totals, explicit exclusions and current validity.
Fulfillment qualityConfirmation latency per component, partial failures, duplicate reservation incidents and inventory discrepancies.
Sales effectivenessQualified leads, quote turnaround, approval-to-confirmation conversion and overdue follow-ups; quoted value is distinct from revenue.
Recommendation qualityUser acceptance, preference fit and corrected intent rate by purpose; evaluate against reviewed scenarios, not engagement alone.

The six purposes can be multi-selected. Shared context and one component cart prevent six independent trip plans. Family plus relaxation combines occupancy and a slower pace; pilgrimage plus rest protects visit timing and downtime; celebrations plus exploration shares the main stay and keeps the celebration proposal separate. Ask for a priority only when actual constraints conflict.

Deduplicate the same supplier product, service date, slot and participant coverage. A private package that already includes airport transfers must not add a second transfer. Distinct slots on different dates are separate services. Package exclusions and fixed bundles must remain visible; do not split a supplier bundle unless its contract permits it.

An external timeout means status unknown, not a safe failure. Query the supplier by client reference before retrying a booking. Store attempted and provider references, surface the uncertainty to operations and suppress duplicate mutations. Search results can be stale; revalidate rate, availability, baggage/meal and cancellation rules before customer commitment.

If one supplier fails after another confirms, retain valid confirmations, show partial completion and request an explicit alternative or compensation decision. Cancellations follow supplier policy; an inventory release, cancellation request and financial refund are different events. An expired quote blocks new commitments but must not prevent truthful reconciliation of an existing supplier response.

11 Group and occasion business rules

  • A group request records route, dates, preferred mode, package, interested count, target size, package amount, price basis and supplier discount tiers where available.
  • Creating a group prepares an invitation link and share preview. Sharing is customer initiated; do not send a message, publish a post or expose participant identities automatically.
  • Joining interest requires appropriate contact/participation consent. Count interested, accepted and paid/confirmed travelers separately. Discount thresholds need verified supplier terms, validity and a revised price acceptance if totals change.
  • A birthday or anniversary budget remains outside the main trip total. Capture requested scope early, but create/confirm the separate arrangement proposal only after an eligible main stay/package is supplier-confirmed.
  • An occasion provider may use the partner booking ledger for the separately approved arrangement. The main-trip acceptance and inventory do not authorize extra charges.

14 Context data flow

Customers and organizers supply accepted requirements and commercial decisions. Partners supply product/rate/availability updates and responses. Staff qualify leads, revise proposals and record evidence. GDS/direct adapters return timestamped offers and booking events. A payment provider returns signed financial events once checkout is implemented. Each external response is validated before it affects a business state.

Do not send unrestricted traveler profiles to the AI gateway or external supplier search. Use purpose, dates, route and necessary party constraints for planning; disclose personal data to fulfillment suppliers only when needed and authorized. The central record connects the trip, quote, approval and component booking references.

16 Partner and booking data flow

Rate publication creates a new catalog revision. Sales can stage a candidate component from dated rates, review inclusions/terms and publish a fresh commercial version. An approved task is then linked to service dates and units. For internal inventory, capacity check, allocation, booking-state update and outbox event must commit together. For external inventory, persist intent and idempotency references before calling the adapter; a provider reply then advances the state.

The local prototype models one activity slot per listing per day and simple room categories. Production must model room occupancy/rate plans and multiple activity sessions explicitly. Cross-tab localStorage is not a database transaction; production concurrency acceptance requires parallel server tests.

Use a server-side provider-agnostic model gateway. The existing proposed Trip Intent Schema v6 is a starting contract and must be versioned with provenance, segment and scope changes. Structured output is validated before use; it is not proof that a date, amenity or price is true. Supplier/user text is untrusted data and cannot redefine tools or instructions. Evaluation scenarios must include mixed purposes, ambiguous dates, missing staff counts, own travel/stay, stale rates, package overlap, low confidence, prompt injection inside a listing and unsupported routes. On model/provider failure keep the accepted context and offer explicit chips or human assistance. Do not fabricate fresh results or a successful booking.

20 Supply adapter and orchestration specification

Define one capability contract for search, quote/revalidate, availability, hold (optional), book, status, amend (optional), cancel (optional), policy and webhook verification. Normalize supplier product/offer/client references and service dates; preserve raw policy references for audit. Declare unsupported capabilities rather than inventing a hold or cancel operation.

Use an orchestration/saga workflow across suppliers. Internal transactions protect ELate allocations, booking state and outbox. They do not make external flight, bus and hotel bookings atomic. Reconciliation workers detect missing callbacks and capacity drift, escalating an assigned task instead of silently changing a customer plan.
